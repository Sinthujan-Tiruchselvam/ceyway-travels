The Island, In Nine Chapters

Where Ceyway takes you

Every itinerary is built from real places we know well — not a checklist. Here are the chapters our travellers return to again and again.

01 — CULTURAL TRIANGLE

Sigiriya & Dambulla

Climb the lion-carved rock fortress at dawn, then walk cave temples painted 2,000 years ago.

02 — HILL COUNTRY

Ella & Nine Arch Bridge

Blue-line trains thread through tea estates and cloud forest on the world's most scenic rail line.

03 — WILDLIFE

Yala & Wilpattu

Sri Lanka has the highest density of leopards on earth — plus elephants, sloth bears and wild peacocks.

04 — COLONIAL COAST

Galle Fort

Dutch ramparts, rampart sunsets, and a walkable old town of boutique villas and courtyard cafés.

05 — SOUTH COAST

Mirissa & Weligama

Blue whales offshore by morning, palm-lined surf breaks and beach shacks by afternoon.

06 — SACRED KANDY

Temple of the Tooth

Sri Lanka's cultural capital, ringed by lake, hills and the island's most revered Buddhist relic.

07 — TEA COUNTRY

Nuwara Eliya

Little England in the clouds — rolling tea gardens, strawberry farms and crisp highland air.

08 — ANCIENT CITIES

Polonnaruwa & Anuradhapura

Vast stupas and stone Buddhas rising out of jungle — Sri Lanka's first two royal capitals.

09 — EAST COAST

Trincomalee & Kalkudah

The road less travelled: quiet coral bays, calm swimming waters and near-empty golden beaches.
